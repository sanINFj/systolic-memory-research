A further static capacity validation was performed across all 11 workloads and four bank-mapping policies, resulting in 44 configurations. For each configuration, the A and B operand addresses were generated using the corresponding bank-mapping equations and checked against the physical bank limits. All 44 configurations satisfied the 8 KiB per-bank capacity constraint and the 13-bit local-address range. The maximum observed bank occupancy was 4 KiB. This validation strengthens the aggregate scratchpad-capacity analysis by explicitly checking individual bank footprints; it does not constitute runtime SRAM or physical implementation validation.
